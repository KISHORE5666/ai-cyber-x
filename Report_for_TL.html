Overview

Phase 2 of AI-CYBER-X covers identity management, zero trust enforcement, and high-level threat telemetry visualization. This report covers real, working progress on two core visualization components of that scope — the Zero Trust Identity & Access Management module and the Executive Security Dashboard.

Rather than reporting only design intentions, this document describes the live dashboards actively monitoring our enterprise environment, correlating real-time events from the SIEM platform, AI Threat Detection, and Identity providers into a single operational view.

Objective of These Components

  • Ingest real-time identity telemetry (RBAC, ABAC, MFA, SSO, Geo-Fencing) to provide a centralized view of user risk and access policies.
  • Surface active threats, blocked threats, and AI detections across 7 ML models into an executive-level summary.
  • Monitor critical endpoint health, open vulnerabilities, and cloud environment findings.
  • Correlate User Behavior Analytics (UBA) anomalies to automatically flag or suspend high-risk users.

Design and Architecture

The platform is structured as modular dashboard views feeding from a common telemetry pipeline.

Enforces Phase 2 Zero Trust Architecture. It evaluates real-time risk scores and applies automated policies such as geo-blocks and session flagging.
